How to Play

🎮 Real vs AI - Can you spot the difference?

BrainPlay AI is a fun, brain-challenging game that tests your ability to distinguish between REAL photographs and AI-generated images. Each round presents 5 images. Your goal? Decide if each image is REAL or AI before time runs out (mentally — there's no timer, but think fast!).

📋 Game Rules

  • You get 5 images per game session.
  • For each image, tap REAL if you think it's an authentic photograph.
  • Tap AI if you believe the image was generated by artificial intelligence.
  • After each answer, you'll see if you were correct — with detailed feedback.
  • Press NEXT to continue to the next image.
  • At the end, your final score (out of 5) will be displayed with a special scorecard.
  • Share your score with friends via WhatsApp, Facebook, or copy the link!

🎯 Step-by-Step Guide

1 Look at the Image Carefully

An image will appear in the main box. Observe details, lighting, textures, and any unusual patterns.

2 Make Your Choice

Tap the REAL button (green) or AI button (red) below the image.

3 Review the Result

A result card will show ✅ CORRECT or ❌ WRONG, plus an explanation of why the image is real or AI-generated.

4 Proceed to Next Image

Click the NEXT >> button to continue. The counter (1/5, 2/5, etc.) shows your progress.

5 Get Your Final Score

After all 5 questions, you'll see your Score Card with emoji rating (🤯 Brilliant, 🔥 Excellent, etc.). You can play again or share your achievement!

💡 Tips to Win

👁️ Look at fingers and eyes — AI often struggles with realistic hands
🌓 Check shadows and reflections — inconsistent lighting is a red flag
🌀 Notice strange textures — AI can create blurry or repeating patterns
🖼️ Examine backgrounds — unnatural objects or distorted shapes
🔍 Zoom in mentally — think about small details like hair strands
🎨 Look for smoothness — AI images sometimes look "too perfect"

🌟 Why Play This Game?

  • Improve brain skills — sharpen observation and critical thinking
  • Learn AI vs real images — become better at detecting deepfakes
  • Fun and quick challenge — perfect for short breaks
  • Share with friends — compare scores and compete

  • 📊 Score tracking — see your performance in real-time

  • 🔄 Play again anytime — restart button available on the score card
  • 📤 Social sharing — post your score on WhatsApp and Facebook
